Our Mission

An Evening on the Water for a Great Cause

Gather with neighbors and friends at the historic Annapolis Marina. Enjoy cool autumn bay breezes, soulful acoustic melodies, and a vibrant community spirit while empowering local teachers and students.

🎸

Live Waterfront Music

Local Chesapeake bay musicians perform acoustic sets right on the pier as the sun sets over docked sailboats.

🏆

Silent Charity Auction

Bid on sailing excursions, weekend getaways, local restaurant baskets, and artwork donated by community artisans.

🥂

Local Bites & Craft Drinks

Savor Maryland crab dip, local oysters, seasonal craft beers, and cider provided by our generous local culinary partners.

🎓

Annapolis Schools Fund

Every bid and donation directly finances classroom supplies, science kits, robotics clubs, and music instruments.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Boatyard Bash free to attend? +
Yes! Admission is 100% free with an RSVP. Food and drinks are provided, and donations/auction bids are voluntary to support our schools.
Where do I park at the Annapolis Marina? +
Designated attendee parking is available at the Annapolis Marina lot and adjacent public garages on Compromise Street with shuttle service.
What is the dress code? +
Smart casual / maritime casual. We recommend comfortable flat shoes for walking on the wooden docks, and a light jacket for the waterfront breeze.
Can I bring my family and children? +
Yes! The event is family-friendly and open to all ages who wish to celebrate Annapolis education.
